## collection
A design point to keep in mind: the two embedders produce vectors of different sizes (384 for MiniLM, 1536 for OpenAI).
A single collection can only hold one size, and vectors from different models can't be compared anyway. 
So we'll create one collection per embedding model, for example bain_minilm and bain_openai.
